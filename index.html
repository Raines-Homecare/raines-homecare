About Me

Care that is personal, respectful and reassuring.

Hi, I'm Lorraine, the founder of Raines Homecare.

I have 14 years of experience in care, including 7 years working within the NHS. During this time, I worked across a range of clinical settings, including Outpatients, the Acute Medical Unit, Emergency, Stroke and Trauma.

Since joining a care agency in 2022, I have continued to build my experience across care homes, supported living and community care, supporting people with a wide range of needs including dementia, Parkinson's disease, acquired brain injuries, cerebral palsy, learning disabilities, autism and mental health conditions.

In 2024, I completed a Manual Handling Train the Trainer course, further developing my knowledge of safe moving and handling.

I believe good care is about compassion, dignity, respect and supporting people to remain as independent as possible. I created Raines Homecare to provide personal, reliable care that is centred around each individual and delivered in the comfort of their own home.
